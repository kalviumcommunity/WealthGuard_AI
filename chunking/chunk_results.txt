WEALTHGUARD AI - TOKEN-AWARE CHUNKING
============================================================

TASK 1 - TOKEN-BASED CHUNKING
------------------------------------------------------------
Tokenizer: tiktoken o200k_base
Chunk size: 256 tokens
Overlap: 40 tokens

Sample input:
Total tokens: 140
Number of chunks: 1

The chunker measures text using tokens instead of characters.
Each chunk contains at most 256 tokens.

TASK 2 - CONTROLLED OVERLAP
------------------------------------------------------------
Each new chunk starts 40 tokens before the previous chunk ends.
Therefore, adjacent chunks share controlled boundary context.

TASK 4 - SIZE AND OVERLAP JUSTIFICATION
------------------------------------------------------------
Chunk size: 256 tokens
Overlap: 40 tokens

256 tokens provides focused retrieval units while leaving sufficient
room for the question, instructions, retrieved chunks, and generated
answer.

40-token overlap is approximately 16% of the chunk size. It preserves
boundary context without excessive duplication and unnecessary cost.

The final RAG prompt must fit within the deployed model's context
window, including instructions, question, retrieved chunks, and
output tokens.

These settings balance retrieval precision, context preservation,
and token cost.
