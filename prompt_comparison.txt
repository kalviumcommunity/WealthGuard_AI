======================================================================
WEALTHGUARD AI - PROMPT COMPARISON
======================================================================

USER QUESTION:
What should a relationship manager consider when explaining an investment product to a customer?

======================================================================
PROMPT VARIATION 1 - VAGUE
======================================================================

SYSTEM PROMPT:
You are a wealth management assistant.
Answer the user's question.

MODEL OUTPUT:
A relationship manager should explain an investment product in a way that is **clear, balanced, and appropriate for the customer**, considering:

1. **Customer objectives and circumstances**
   - Investment goals, time horizon, income needs, existing assets, knowledge, experience, and risk capacity.
   - Whether the product is suitable for the customer’s objectives and financial situation.

2. **How the product works**
   - Underlying assets, investment strategy, expected return drivers, term, and how gains or losses arise.
   - Any conditions, triggers, barriers, guarantees, or complex features.

3. **Risks**
   - Possibility of losing some or all of the investment.
   - Market, credit, interest-rate, currency, inflation, liquidity, concentration, and issuer risks, as relevant.
   - Explain that past performance is not a reliable indicator of future results.

4. **Costs and charges**
   - Entry and exit fees, management fees, commissions, spreads, performance fees, penalties, and any indirect costs.
   - Explain how charges affect returns.

5. **Liquidity and access to funds**
   - Whether the customer can sell or withdraw early, how long this may take, and whether early exit could result in losses or penalties.

6. **Tax considerations**
   - Potential tax treatment, while making clear that tax outcomes depend on the customer’s circumstances and may change.

7. **Alternatives and conflicts**
   - Relevant alternatives and why this product may or may not be appropriate.
   - Any commissions, incentives, affiliations, or other conflicts of interest.

8. **Communication and understanding**
   - Use plain language, avoid jargon and exaggerated claims, and present benefits and risks fairly.
   - Check the customer understands—especially downside scenarios—rather than simply asking whether they agree.

9. **Regulatory and documentation requirements**
   - Provide required disclosures, key information documents, terms and conditions, and suitability or appropriateness assessments.
   - Keep an accurate record of the discussion and the customer’s consent.

The key principle is: **do not just describe the product; explain whether and why it may be suitable for that particular customer, including its worst-case outcomes and total costs.**

======================================================================
PROMPT VARIATION 2 - CLEAR AND CONSTRAINED
======================================================================

SYSTEM PROMPT:
You are WealthGuard AI, an evidence-based assistant for relationship managers.

Scope:
- Provide general informational guidance based on approved wealth-management knowledge.
- Explain concepts clearly without making personalized financial recommendations.
- Do not invent facts, regulations, product features, tax treatment, or eligibility requirements.
- If the required information is unavailable or cannot be verified, say:
  "I don't have enough verified information to answer this reliably."

Constraints:
- Keep the answer under 120 words.
- Use a professional and neutral tone.
- Structure the response as 3-5 concise bullet points.
- Clearly distinguish general information from customer-specific advice.

MODEL OUTPUT:
- Explain the product’s objectives, strategy, structure, liquidity, time horizon, and key risks in clear, balanced language.  
- Disclose fees, charges, potential conflicts of interest, performance variability, and the possibility of losing some or all invested capital.  
- Check that the explanation is consistent with the customer’s knowledge, experience, objectives, risk tolerance, financial circumstances, and applicable eligibility requirements.  
- Avoid guarantees or misleading comparisons; distinguish historical performance from expected results.  
- Document the discussion, materials provided, customer questions, and any required disclosures or suitability assessments.

======================================================================
CHOSEN PROMPT
======================================================================

Variation 2 - Clear and constrained

Why it works better:
- Clearly defines the assistant's role and scope.
- Prevents unsupported or invented financial information.
- Sets a professional and neutral tone.
- Limits response length for easier review.
- Requires a consistent bullet-point format.
- Explicitly defines what to say when verified information is unavailable.
- Keeps customer-specific financial decisions with qualified professionals.
